Why this exists

The field needed a map. The board needs one too.

Until now, the US video game education pipeline has been documented in pieces. A press release here. A Princeton Review list there. No single, maintained, donor-facing artifact connects the elementary entry program in Atlanta to the graduate research lab in Pittsburgh to the GGP Scholar to the Take-Two ERG mentor who took the call. This directory is that artifact.

Built as a public good. Maintained as an obligation. Anchored by Take-Two Interactive as the launch partner who made the field navigable.

Donor Lens · AI Readiness

The highest-leverage tier in the directory. The curriculum gap is acute and current. A scholar trained in AI-tool fluency entering the games industry in 2027 is a different hire profile than one without. Cost-per-outcome is low because most existing scholarship infrastructure can absorb an AI module without rebuilding.

Use-case framework

Two lenses through this directory.

Built for students and families AND for donors. Each constituency reads the same data through a different filter.

For a student or family

"I'm a [age] in [region]. What's available to me?"
  • Age: 5-10 / 11-14 / 14-18 / 18-22 / 22+
  • Region: West / Mountain / Midwest / South / Northeast / Texas / HBCU
  • Cost tier: Free / sliding scale / tuition + scholarship / full pay
  • Audience served: Open / women+girls / underestimated communities / disability / first-gen / HBCU
  • Time-of-year: Summer-only / year-round / academic-year-aligned

For a donor or funder

"I want to fund [outcome]. Where does my dollar go furthest?"
  • Outcomes data: completion rate, placement, industry hire rate
  • Cost-per-outcome: $/student exposed, $/scholarship recipient, $/industry hire
  • Pipeline leakage points: end of MS, end of HS, after 2nd year of college
  • Match-making: Funder's priorities → programs that already serve that population
  • Readiness layer: which programs include AI fluency vs. which would benefit from an AI overlay

For GGP specifically

"Where does GGP add unique value, distinct from existing programs?"

GGP's edge is mentorship at scale plus industry visibility for all students pursuing careers in games, including those from underestimated communities. The directory positions GGP not as a competitor but as the connective tissue across programs · a published, maintained resource that donors fund and students depend on.

Method note

This is a starter directory. Outcomes data is not in this draft for most entries; procuring it is the work the funded build-out delivers. Some 2025-2026 board changes at G4C and other orgs may have shifted; canonical board pages remain the live source of truth. Several mid-tier programs (regional, HBCU, special-needs) are present but light on detail; the funded build-out is what fills these. The full version, post Take-Two Foundation launch partnership, will include 200 to 400 programs with verified outcomes data, hosted at gaygamingpros.org/directory, filterable, searchable, quarterly-refreshed.
