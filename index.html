Why the field needs this

Three problems the Directory solves at once.

Until now, the US video game education pipeline has been documented in pieces. A press release here. A Princeton Review list there. No single, maintained, donor-facing artifact connects the elementary entry program in Atlanta to the graduate research lab in Pittsburgh to the GGP Scholar to the Take-Two ERG mentor who took the call.

Students get lost

A family in Memphis has no canonical place to learn what is available to their kid in middle school. Pipeline leakage starts in K-8.

Donors guess

Companies who want to fund the games-education pipeline make decisions on press releases and personal networks. There is no donor-facing reference with cost-per-outcome data.

The field is fragmented

Black Girls CODE, Code.org, Gameheads, Girls Make Games, GGP, Games for Change, IGNITE, Princeton Review's top schools list. All real, all good, all uncoordinated. No artifact connects them.
